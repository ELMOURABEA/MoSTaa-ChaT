# MoSTaa-ChaT v1.0.0 - Feature Map & Release Strategy

## Product Overview

**MoSTaa-ChaT v1.0.0** is an enterprise-grade omnichannel communications platform designed for healthcare providers, retail businesses, and large enterprises. It unifies WhatsApp, chat, voice, video, AI, and social media communications into a single, professional business operational system.

### Product Vision
Transform business communications from fragmented, channel-specific systems into one unified omnichannel platform that enables real-time customer engagement, team collaboration, and data-driven decision-making.

### Target Markets
- Healthcare Systems & Clinics
- Retail & E-commerce
- Financial Services
- Customer Support Centers
- Enterprise B2B Operations
- Government/Public Services
